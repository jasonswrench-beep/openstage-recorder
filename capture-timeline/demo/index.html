OpenStage Capture: clip list demo

This is a demo. It starts with a made-up lesson. Nothing is recorded, and no file on your computer is changed unless you choose Save. You can open an edit file, or a recording folder made by OpenStage Capture (the one that ends in .capture), and play it.

The preview, the timeline and the clip list all show the same edit and make the same changes. Choose a clip with the arrow keys, press S to split it at the playhead, Space to play, or open Keyboard shortcuts under the timeline or the list for every key. F6 moves between the three.
